FOR AMMA — A LETTER MADE OF LIGHT
==================================

To finish the card, save the 3 photos you sent into THIS folder
with these exact filenames:

  1. photo-waterfall.jpg   ← the waterfall hug photo
  2. photo-eating.jpg      ← amma eating, smiling
  3. photo-sleeping.jpg    ← amma resting with the koala

Then open index.html in any browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.)
and tap to begin.

THE EXPERIENCE
---------------
7 scenes unfold one tap at a time:
  0. Welcome — a glowing orb
  1. The Climbs (waterfall)
  2. The Small Joys (sandwich)
  3. When I Broke
  4. Everything You Did (the care list)
  5. While You Slept
  6. The Constellation — tap the night sky 21 times,
     each tap places a star + a thank-you, and they
     form a heart in the sky
  7. The Letter — handwritten letter + photos +
     a "send love" button that bursts hearts

WAYS TO SHARE IT
-----------------
• Open it on your laptop and let her experience it
  right there with you
• Screen-record yourself walking her through it
  and send the video
• Host it on Netlify drop / GitHub Pages and send a
  link (drag this whole folder onto netlify.com/drop)

THE LETTER IS EDITABLE
-----------------------
Open index.html in any text editor. Search for
"Dear Amma," — the letter is right there. Adjust
anything that doesn't sound like you. Add details only
the two of you would know. Make it yours.

Same goes for the 21 thank-yous on the constellation —
search for "THANK_YOUS = [" and edit any line.
